Get Started

Install in under 2 minutes

No App Store. Load it directly in Chrome as an unpacked extension — it's simpler than it sounds.

Step 0 — Download the Extension

Get the latest ZIP file from Krutika or download it below.

Download ZIP
1

Unzip the downloaded file

Double-click the ZIP to extract it. You'll get a folder called PSELearningHub-Extension — keep this folder somewhere easy to find (e.g. your Desktop or Documents).

Do not move or delete this folder after installing — Chrome loads from it directly.
2

Open Chrome Extensions page

In Chrome, copy and paste this URL into the address bar and press Enter:

chrome://extensions
3

Turn on Developer Mode

In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle on Developer mode. Three new buttons will appear — "Load unpacked", "Pack extension", "Update".

4

Load the unpacked extension

Click Load unpacked, then in the file picker, navigate to and select the PSELearningHub-Extension folder you unzipped in Step 1. Click Select.

The PSE Learning Hub icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar.
5

Pin the extension (optional but recommended)

Click the puzzle piece icon 🧩 in the Chrome toolbar, find PSE Learning Hub, and click the pin icon to keep it always visible in the toolbar.

6

Open and you're done!

Click the PSE Learning Hub icon in your toolbar. It will sign you in automatically using your Chrome Salesforce account. You should see your customer realms appear within a few seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I see my customer realms after installing?
Make sure you're signed into Chrome with your Salesforce Google account. The extension uses your Chrome profile identity to identify you. If you're signed in with a personal Gmail, switch to your Salesforce account in Chrome settings.
Do I need to do anything when there's a new version?
Yes — download the new ZIP, unzip it, then go to chrome://extensions, find PSE Learning Hub, and click Remove. Then repeat steps 3–4 above with the new folder.
Will my uploads be visible to the whole team?
Yes. All uploads go into the team's Google Shared Drive and are registered in a central registry. Any team member who has the extension installed will see your upload the next time they open it.
What file types can I upload?
Any file type — PDF, PPTX, DOCX, XLSX, MP4, PNG, etc. You can also add external links (like a Grafana URL or Confluence page) instead of uploading a file.
Can I delete someone else's document?
No. The delete button only appears on documents you uploaded. This prevents accidental deletion of other team members' work.
